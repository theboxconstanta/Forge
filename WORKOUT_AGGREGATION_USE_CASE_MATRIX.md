# Workout Aggregation — Use Case Matrix

**Status:** Research artifact, companion to `WORKOUT_AGGREGATION_ARCHITECTURE.md`. No code, schema, or migration implied by this document.

Every case is modeled against the architecture document's two-family taxonomy: **Family A (value-combine)** — `sum | best-of | average | max | min` over Section Results sharing the same metric kind, canonical unit, and comparator direction; **Family B (rank-combine)** — `placement-sum | points-sum` over Section Results' own already-computed ranks, usable even when Sections are metric-incompatible. `NONE` (no `aggregateDefinition` at all) is the default for every Workout, single- or multi-Section, unless a coach explicitly configures one.

Columns: **Section scores** (what each participating Section is scored as) · **Aggregate exists?** · **Type** (NONE / A-sum / A-best-of / A-average / B-placement-sum / B-points-sum) · **Inputs** (which Sections/values participate) · **Output** · **Comparator** (inherited from the combine type, never separately configured) · **Missing-data behavior** · **Tie behavior** · **Rx behavior** · **Domain** (confirms which owning boundary the case belongs to — Workout Aggregation, or explicitly something else: Segment, Attempt, Competition Mode, Team/Partner).
